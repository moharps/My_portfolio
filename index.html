Who is Mohamed Hassan Abdi?

I am a third-year Computer Science student at the University of Embu and a web and software developer, also known as Moharps Tech. I build practical web applications that solve real problems in Kenya, and I led the development of AgriConnect, a platform that links Embu County farmers to local buyers.

My work focuses on web development, Python and Flask applications, databases, and secure, responsive interfaces. I have built tools for petrol station reconciliation and for connecting fundis to property managers. I work well in teams, take ownership of delivery, and I am looking for an internship or junior role where I can learn from an engineering team and ship software that matters.
